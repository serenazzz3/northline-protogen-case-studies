WHAT THE DATA SAYS

Line B moved from 5.1% in February to 1.6% in June — the steepest change in the plant.

03 / THE TAKEAWAY

The improvement has an address.

Line B accounts for most of the visible drop after March. The inspection step is a useful point to investigate further, alongside staffing, mix, and process conditions.

The next decision is not simply “keep improving.” It is to learn what changed on Line B and test which parts can transfer to the other lines.
